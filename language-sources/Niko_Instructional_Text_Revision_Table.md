**重点：AO1 术语辨认＋AO2 结合读者与目的解释效果**

**★ 标记老师“What to expect”列出的全部23项；后面10项为不加星的词类补充。** 老师把 minor sentence / elliptical sentence 合写为一项，本表保留这一安排并说明二者区别。主表按老师原清单顺序排列。

第3列是常见的分析方向，第5列是针对该例句的英文分析与中文解释。例句均为自编的书面操作指南／安全说明情境；分析以读者能自行完成操作为背景，需要时明确初学者这一受众。实际考试应按题目给定文本调整。

“Level 5解释”展示准确术语、具体引文、意义与语境之间的联系；Level 5是整体答卷的评价，不能由某一句话单独保证。本次每题2分，可从示范中选取最相关的一两句作答。学习重点是解释词语和结构如何共同服务目的，避免机械套用固定效果。

| **★ Co-ordinating conjunction｜并列连词** | **EN:** A word that links grammatically equal words, phrases or clauses.<br>**中文：**连接地位相同的词、短语或分句；常见 and、but、or。注意：出现 and 不一定就是并列句，还要看是否连接独立分句。 | **EN:** Links actions, alternatives or contrasts.<br>**中文：**连接动作、提供选项或说明对比；具体作用取决于连词及语境。 | **EN:** You can use batteries **or** connect the mains adapter.<br>**中文：**你可以使用电池，**或者**连接电源适配器。 | **EN:** The co-ordinating conjunction “or” presents two alternative power sources. This allows users to select the option available to them, making the instructions usable in different situations rather than implying that both sources are required.<br>**中文：**“or”把两种供电方式列为二选一。读者可以按现有条件选择，避免误以为两种电源都要接上，使说明适用于不同使用情境。 |
| **★ Subordinating conjunction｜从属连词** | **EN:** A word that introduces a subordinate clause and signals its relationship to the main clause.<br>**中文：**引出从句，并说明它与主句的条件、时间、原因等关系；如 if、when、because、although。 | **EN:** Makes conditions, timing or reasons explicit.<br>**中文：**明确某一步在什么条件下、什么时候、为什么执行。 | **EN:** **If** the warning light flashes, switch off the machine.<br>**中文：****如果**警示灯闪烁，就关闭机器。 | **EN:** The subordinating conjunction “if” makes switching off the machine conditional on a visible warning signal. Readers can therefore recognise when the instruction applies, supporting safe operation without suggesting that the machine must always remain off.<br>**中文：**“if”把关机与可观察到的警示信号联系起来。读者能够判断何时需要执行这条安全指令，而不会理解成机器必须一直关闭。 |
| **★ Relative pronoun｜关系代词** | **EN:** A pronoun that introduces a relative clause, refers to an antecedent and has a grammatical role within that clause.<br>**中文：**引出关系从句，回指前面的名词，并在从句中充当主语或宾语等成分；按本次课堂语法体系识别 who、which、that。 | **EN:** Links an object to identifying or additional information.<br>**中文：**把物件与限定或补充它的信息连接起来，帮助辨认。 | **EN:** Use the cable **that** came with the device.<br>**中文：**使用设备附带的那条线。 | **EN:** The relative pronoun “that” refers back to “the cable” and acts as the subject of “came”. It links the cable to its identifying detail, helping users select the supplied cable and reducing the risk of using an incompatible replacement.<br>**中文：**“that”回指 cable，并在从句中作 came 的主语。它引出“设备附带的”这一辨认依据，帮助读者选对线缆，减少使用不兼容替代品的风险。 |
| **★ Discourse markers｜语篇标记** | **EN:** Words or phrases that organise a text or signal relationships between parts of it.<br>**中文：**提示文本顺序或思路关系的词语，如 first、next、finally。它是语篇功能名称，不是单一词类。 | **EN:** Guides readers through the sequence or organisation.<br>**中文：**帮助读者跟随步骤，了解当前进度及先后顺序。 | **EN:** **Finally**, replace the cover before switching on the device.<br>**中文：****最后**，先盖回外盖，再开启设备。 | **EN:** The discourse marker “finally” signals that replacing the cover is the last stage of the procedure. It helps beginners track their progress, while the following timing instruction makes clear that the device should only be switched on after reassembly.<br>**中文：**“finally”标明装回外盖是流程的最后阶段，帮助初学者确认进度；后面的时间要求又明确说明，应先完成重新组装，再开机。 |
| **★ Noun clause｜名词性从句** | **EN:** A subordinate clause functioning in a position typically occupied by a noun phrase, such as subject or object.<br>**中文：**整个从句承担类似名词短语的工作，例如作主语或宾语。判断关键是整个从句在大句中干什么。 | **EN:** Specifies the information to check, know or establish.<br>**中文：**准确说明要检查、确认或了解的内容。 | **EN:** Check **whether the indicator light is on**.<br>**中文：**检查**指示灯是否亮着**。 | **EN:** The noun clause “whether the indicator light is on” functions as the object of “check”. It specifies the exact information the reader must establish, turning a vague request to check the device into an observable diagnostic task.<br>**中文：**整个 whether 从句是 check 的宾语，明确规定要确认的信息。读者需要检查一个可观察的状态，而不是笼统地“看看设备有没有问题”。 |
| **★ Subordinate clause｜从属分句／从句** | **EN:** A clause that is grammatically dependent on a main clause or embedded within a larger structure.<br>**中文：**在语法上依附于主句或嵌入更大结构的分句。关系从句、名词性从句等都可属于 subordinate clause；题目要具体名称时，应进一步细分。 | **EN:** Adds a dependent condition, time, reason or other detail.<br>**中文：**把条件、时间、原因等信息明确附加到主要动作上。 | **EN:** **Before you remove the cover**, disconnect the power.<br>**中文：****在拆下外盖之前**，先断开电源。 | **EN:** The initial subordinate clause “Before you remove the cover” establishes a required order between the actions. Placing it first alerts readers to the safety condition before they reach the instruction, reducing the risk of opening a powered device.<br>**中文：**句首从句明确规定两个动作的顺序，并让读者先看到安全条件，再读到主要指令，减少带电拆机的风险。其作用来自 before 表达的时间关系及从句的位置。 |
| **★ Relative clause｜关系从句／定语从句** | **EN:** A subordinate clause that modifies a noun or noun phrase by identifying it or adding information about it.<br>**中文：**修饰名词，说明“是哪一个”，或者补充有关信息。注意区分整个 relative clause 和引出它的 relative pronoun。 | **EN:** Identifies the correct item or supplies relevant detail.<br>**中文：**缩小所指范围，帮助读者在多个物件中选对对象。 | **EN:** Press the button **that is marked START**.<br>**中文：**按下**标有 START 的**按钮。 | **EN:** The defining relative clause “that is marked START” restricts “the button” to the one carrying a particular label. For a reader facing several controls, this provides a visible selection criterion and reduces the likelihood of pressing the wrong button.<br>**中文：**这个限定性关系从句把 button 限定为标有 START 的那一个。面对多个按键的读者，可以用可见标签作判断，减少按错的可能。 |
| **★ Minor sentence / elliptical sentence｜小句／省略句** | **EN:** A minor sentence lacks the usual full clause structure; an elliptical sentence omits material recoverable from context.<br>**中文：**Minor sentence 不具备通常完整的分句结构；elliptical sentence 省略语境中可补出的内容。两者可能重合，但不完全同义。正常祈使句也不能仅因未出现 you 就判为 minor sentence。 | **EN:** Can create compact labels, warnings or checks.<br>**中文：**适合简短标签、警告或检查提示，但简短也可能牺牲解释细节。 | **EN:** A. **Safety first.**<br>B. **Ready?** [Are you ready?]<br>**中文：**A. **安全第一。**（minor sentence）<br>B. **准备好了吗？**（可补出 Are you ready?，属于 ellipsis） | **EN:** “Safety first” is a minor sentence that presents safety as an immediately visible priority. “Ready?” elliptically omits “Are you”, creating a brief readiness check that may prompt readers to pause before continuing. Their functions depend on where they appear in the procedure.<br>**中文：**“Safety first”用简短小句突出安全这一首要考虑；“Ready?”省略 Are you，形成简洁的准备情况检查，可能促使读者暂停确认。具体作用取决于它们出现的位置。 |
| **★ Non-finite verb｜非限定动词** | **EN:** A verb form that is not itself marked for tense, person or number, including infinitives and participles.<br>**中文：**本身不标示时态、人称或数的动词形式，包括不定式和分词。不能只看 -ed／-ing 就判断，要看它在结构中的作用。 | **EN:** Can express purpose or package an action within a larger clause.<br>**中文：**可用于交代目的或把一个动作嵌入另一个结构；作用随具体形式变化。 | **EN:** **To prevent damage**, place the device on a flat surface.<br>**中文：****为防止损坏**，把设备放在平整表面上。 | **EN:** The non-finite infinitive “to prevent” introduces the purpose of the placement instruction. By stating the risk it helps avoid, the text gives users a practical reason to follow the advice rather than presenting the surface requirement as arbitrary.<br>**中文：**非限定不定式 to prevent 引出摆放要求的目的，并点明它要避免的风险。读者因此明白为何需要平整表面，而不只是机械服从一项要求。 |
| **★ Finite verb｜限定动词** | **EN:** A verb form that provides tense or finiteness in a clause and may also show agreement with its subject.<br>**中文：**在分句中承担时态或限定作用的动词，有些形式还与主语的人称和数对应。有助动词时，要找哪一个动词承担限定作用。 | **EN:** Anchors a statement in tense; the specific tense shapes meaning.<br>**中文：**把陈述组织成有时态／限定性的分句；具体效果来自所用时态与语境，而非“限定动词”这个标签本身。 | **EN:** The device **switches** off automatically after ten minutes.<br>**中文：**设备在十分钟后会自动关闭。 | **EN:** The present-tense finite verb “switches” presents automatic shutdown as the device’s normal behaviour. Together with “after ten minutes”, it gives users a predictable operating expectation, helping them distinguish a routine shutdown from a fault.<br>**中文：**现在时限定动词 switches 把自动关机呈现为设备的一般运作方式；配合十分钟这一时间信息，帮助读者形成明确预期，区分正常关机与故障。 |
| **★ Modal verb｜情态动词** | **EN:** An auxiliary verb expressing meanings such as obligation, permission, possibility or ability.<br>**中文：**表达义务、许可、可能性、能力等的助动词，如 must、should、may、can。意义要结合句子判断，不能把 can 永远译成“能力”。 | **EN:** Grades requirements, recommendations or possibilities.<br>**中文：**区分强制要求、建议、许可或可能性，帮助读者判断指令力度。 | **EN:** You **must** disconnect the power before cleaning.<br>**中文：**清洁之前，你**必须**断开电源。 | **EN:** The modal verb “must” expresses strong obligation, making disconnection a compulsory safety step rather than an optional recommendation. In a cleaning guide, this positions the writer as an authority setting a clear boundary around safe user behaviour.<br>**中文：**must 表达强制义务，把断电规定为必须完成的安全步骤。清洁指南由此确立操作规范，也体现作者作为指导者对安全要求的权威。 |
| **★ Active voice｜主动语态** | **EN:** A construction in which the subject is typically the agent performing the action.<br>**中文：**主语通常是动作的执行者。辨认重点是谁在做动作，而不是句子是否短。 | **EN:** Can foreground the actor and responsibility.<br>**中文：**可突出执行者，让操作责任更明确。 | **EN:** **You** attach the cable to the port labelled IN.<br>**中文：****你**把线缆接到标有 IN 的接口上。 | **EN:** The active construction makes “you” the agent of “attach”, explicitly assigning the action to the reader. Combined with the labelled destination, it clarifies both who performs the task and where the cable belongs, supporting independent operation.<br>**中文：**主动结构把 you 设为 attach 的执行者，直接指定读者负责完成动作；再配合接口标签，明确“谁来接、接在哪里”，帮助读者独立操作。 |
| **★ Passive voice｜被动语态** | **EN:** A construction in which the subject typically receives the action, commonly formed with be and a past participle.<br>**中文：**主语通常是动作的承受者，常见 be＋过去分词。执行者可用 by 引出，也可以省略；be 后接形容词不一定是被动。 | **EN:** Can foreground a process or affected object and omit an unnecessary agent.<br>**中文：**可突出操作对象或过程，并省略不重要的执行者。 | **EN:** The cover **is secured** with two screws during assembly.<br>**中文：**组装时，外盖**由两颗螺丝固定**。 | **EN:** The passive construction “is secured” places “the cover” in subject position and leaves the assembler unnamed. This keeps attention on how the component is attached, giving users relevant structural information before they attempt to remove it.<br>**中文：**is secured 把外盖置于主语位置，并省略组装者，使注意力集中在部件如何固定。这为读者随后拆卸外盖提供相关结构信息。 |
| **★ Concrete noun｜具体名词** | **EN:** A noun referring to something perceptible through the senses, rather than an abstract idea.<br>**中文：**指可通过感官感知的事物，例如 button、cable、water；与 abstract noun 对照。 | **EN:** Names tangible objects relevant to the task.<br>**中文：**指明实际操作的物件，让文字与读者面前的物体对应。 | **EN:** Remove the **battery** before opening the cover.<br>**中文：**打开外盖之前，先取出**电池**。 | **EN:** The concrete noun “battery” names a physical component the user can locate and remove. In this safety instruction, it makes the required action practical and identifiable; the instruction’s precision also depends on the surrounding action and timing information.<br>**中文：**battery 指向读者能够找到并取出的实际部件，使安全要求落实到具体对象。清楚的指令来自这个名词与动作、先后顺序信息共同作用。 |
| **★ Subject–verb–object (SVO)｜主语—动词—宾语** | **EN:** A clause pattern in which a subject is followed by a verb and its direct object.<br>**中文：**说明“谁／什么，做什么，作用于谁／什么”。SVO 是分句结构，不是词类；并非所有英语分句都符合 SVO。 | **EN:** Can make the agent–action–object relationship explicit.<br>**中文：**可直接呈现执行者、动作与受影响对象的关系。 | **EN:** **The user** [S] **presses** [V] **the reset button** [O].<br>**中文：****用户**［主语］**按下**［动词］**重置按钮**［宾语］。 | **EN:** The SVO pattern explicitly connects “the user”, “presses” and “the reset button”. This makes the actor and target of the action easy to identify, while “reset” distinguishes the relevant control from other buttons on the device.<br>**中文：**SVO 结构直接连接执行者、按压动作和目标按钮；reset 又进一步限定是哪一个按钮，共同减少操作对象上的歧义。 |
| **★ Complex sentence｜主从复合句** | **EN:** A sentence containing a main clause and at least one subordinate clause.<br>**中文：**含主句和至少一个从句。关键是存在主从关系，不是句子长或看起来复杂。 | **EN:** Integrates an action with a condition, reason or qualification.<br>**中文：**把主要动作与执行条件、原因或限制放在同一句中。 | **EN:** **If the screen freezes**, hold the power button for ten seconds.<br>**中文：****如果屏幕卡住**，按住电源键十秒。 | **EN:** The complex sentence integrates a fault condition with its remedy. The initial “if” clause restricts the ten-second hold to a frozen screen, helping users choose the appropriate response instead of treating it as a routine step for every use.<br>**中文：**这个复合句把故障条件与处理方式整合起来。句首 if 从句限定长按的适用情境，帮助读者在屏幕卡住时采取对应措施，而不是每次使用都长按。 |
| **★ Compound sentence｜并列句** | **EN:** A sentence containing two or more co-ordinated independent clauses.<br>**中文：**包含两个或更多并列的独立分句，各分句通常都能独立成句。仅连接两个词或短语的 and 不足以构成并列句。 | **EN:** Connects related stages, outcomes or contrasting facts.<br>**中文：**将相关阶段、结果或对比信息连接起来；关系取决于具体连词与内容。 | **EN:** **The light turns green**, and **the device is ready for use**.<br>**中文：****指示灯变绿**，而且**设备已可以使用**。 | **EN:** The compound sentence connects two independent clauses with “and”, linking a visible signal to operational readiness. Readers can use the green light as a practical cue for when to begin, supporting the guide’s purpose of enabling correct use.<br>**中文：**and 连接两个独立分句，将可见的绿灯状态与“已可使用”联系起来，给读者一个开始操作的实用判断依据。 |
| **★ Evaluative adjectives｜评价性形容词** | **EN:** Adjectives that express a judgement or assessment, such as easy, useful or excellent.<br>**中文：**表达对事物的判断或评价，例如 easy、useful、excellent；与主要描述颜色、形状等属性的形容词区分。 | **EN:** Frames a task or product through a judgement.<br>**中文：**引导读者怎样评价任务或产品，例如觉得容易、实用或可靠。 | **EN:** This **easy** method helps you replace the filter.<br>**中文：**这种**简单的**方法可以帮助你更换过滤器。 | **EN:** The evaluative adjective “easy” presents filter replacement as manageable. For inexperienced users, this may reduce reluctance to attempt maintenance, although the reassurance depends on the following instructions actually being clear enough to support that claim.<br>**中文：**easy 把更换过滤器评价为容易完成，可能减轻新手尝试维护的顾虑。但这种安抚是否有效，也取决于后续步骤是否真的足够清楚。 |
| **★ Proper nouns｜专有名词** | **EN:** Names identifying particular people, places, organisations, brands or other specific entities.<br>**中文：**特定人、地点、组织、品牌等的名称，通常首字母大写；但句首大写本身不能证明是专有名词。 | **EN:** Identifies a specific named entity relevant to the instructions.<br>**中文：**将指令定位到特定品牌、平台或软件，减少对象上的不确定。 | **EN:** Open **YouTube** and select Settings.<br>**中文：**打开 **YouTube**，然后选择“设置”。 | **EN:** The proper noun “YouTube” identifies the platform to which the instruction applies. This gives readers a specific starting point and prevents a generic reference to “the app” from being confused with another application on their device.<br>**中文：**YouTube 明确规定这条指令针对的平台，让读者知道从哪里开始，避免把笼统的“应用程序”误解为设备上的其他软件。 |
| **★ Verb phrase｜动词短语** | **EN:** A phrase headed by a main verb, which may include auxiliary and modal verbs.<br>**中文：**以主要动词为中心，可包含助动词、情态动词。本表按动词链识别，如 must be connected；后面的宾语不并入此动词链。 | **EN:** Combines action or state with tense, modality, aspect or voice.<br>**中文：**通过动词组合同时表达动作／状态以及义务、时态、体或语态等信息。 | **EN:** The device **must be disconnected** before cleaning.<br>**中文：**清洁之前，设备**必须断开连接**。 | **EN:** The verb phrase “must be disconnected” combines compulsory obligation with passive voice. It foregrounds the required state of the device before cleaning, making the safety condition central even though the person responsible is left implicit.<br>**中文：**这个动词短语将 must 的强制义务与被动语态结合，突出清洁前设备必须达到的状态；即使没有明说执行者，安全条件仍然明确。 |
| **★ Adjective phrase｜形容词短语** | **EN:** A phrase headed by an adjective, possibly with modifiers or complements.<br>**中文：**以形容词为中心，可带程度修饰语或补足成分。如 completely dry 的中心词是 dry。 | **EN:** Describes a property, evaluation or required state more precisely.<br>**中文：**更具体地描述属性、评价或需要达到的状态。 | **EN:** Make sure the surface is **completely dry** before reconnecting the power.<br>**中文：**重新接通电源之前，确保表面**完全干燥**。 | **EN:** The adjective phrase “completely dry”, headed by “dry”, specifies the required condition of the surface. The degree modifier “completely” rules out partial drying, giving readers a stricter safety threshold before they reconnect the power.<br>**中文：**短语以 dry 为中心，明确表面应达到的状态；completely 排除“只干了一部分”的情况，给重新接电设定更严格的安全条件。 |
| **★ Noun phrase｜名词短语** | **EN:** A phrase headed by a noun or pronoun, which may include determiners and modifiers.<br>**中文：**以名词或代词为中心，可包含限定词、前置或后置修饰语。the small red button 整体仍是名词短语，因为中心词是 button。 | **EN:** Identifies and specifies the participant or object.<br>**中文：**通过限定和描述，帮助读者辨认准确的操作对象。 | **EN:** Press **the small red button**.<br>**中文：**按下**那个小红按钮**。 | **EN:** The noun phrase “the small red button” combines a definite article with size and colour modifiers. These visible attributes distinguish the intended control from nearby buttons, helping a beginner locate it without relying on technical knowledge.<br>**中文：**这个名词短语把定冠词与大小、颜色修饰语结合，用读者看得见的特征区分相邻按钮，使初学者无需专业知识也能找到目标。 |
| **★ Prepositional phrase｜介词短语** | **EN:** A phrase consisting of a preposition and its complement, commonly a noun phrase.<br>**中文：**由介词及其补足成分组成，常见“介词＋名词短语”。可表达位置、时间、方式等；under 是介词，under the cover 才是整个介词短语。 | **EN:** Locates an item or specifies a relevant relation.<br>**中文：**交代物件位置或其他关系，为操作提供定位信息。 | **EN:** The reset switch is **under the cover**.<br>**中文：**重置开关位于**外盖下面**。 | **EN:** The prepositional phrase “under the cover” locates the reset switch relative to a visible part of the device. This narrows the reader’s search area and compensates for the writer being unable to point to the component in person.<br>**中文：**这个介词短语用外盖作为参照定位重置开关，缩小读者寻找范围，并弥补书面说明中作者无法当面指给读者看的限制。 |

## 二、直接补充上述考点的10项常用词类

| 术语 | 解释（英文解释＋中文通俗解释） | effect | 例子（英文例子＋中文翻译） | 解释例句的effect（Level 5解释） |

| **Abstract noun｜抽象名词** | **EN:** A noun referring to an idea, quality, feeling or state rather than a concrete entity.<br>**中文：**指概念、品质、感受或状态，如 safety、confidence、damage。与老师清单中的 concrete noun 直接对照；老师前面的示例也提到 abstract noun。 | **EN:** Can foreground an underlying value, quality or outcome.<br>**中文：**可突出操作背后的价值、品质或结果；具体效果取决于名词的意义。 | **EN:** For your **safety**, disconnect the power before cleaning.<br>**中文：**为保障你的**安全**，清洁之前请断开电源。 | **EN:** The abstract noun “safety” frames disconnection as protection of the reader rather than an unexplained technical requirement. Combined with “your”, it personalises the reason for compliance and supports the guide’s aim of preventing harm during cleaning.<br>**中文：**safety 把断电要求解释为保护读者，而不是一条没有理由的技术规定；配合 your，安全理由直接关联读者本人，服务于预防清洁伤害的目的。 |
| **Common noun｜普通名词** | **EN:** A noun naming a general class of people, places or things, rather than a particular named entity.<br>**中文：**指某一类人、地点或事物的一般名称，如 user、screen、cloth；与 proper noun 对照。common／proper 与 concrete／abstract 是不同分类维度。 | **EN:** Names a category of item or participant.<br>**中文：**指明一类物件或参与者；是否精确还取决于修饰语和上下文。 | **EN:** Use a soft **cloth** to clean the screen.<br>**中文：**使用一块柔软的**布**清洁屏幕。 | **EN:** The common noun “cloth” identifies a category of cleaning material without naming a branded product. Together with the modifier “soft”, it gives users a practical selection criterion, balancing flexibility over what they use with protection of the screen.<br>**中文：**cloth 指定清洁材料的类别，没有限定某个品牌；soft 再给出选择条件，让读者既有材料选择的余地，也能注意保护屏幕。 |
| **Adverb｜副词** | **EN:** A word that modifies a verb, adjective, another adverb or a whole clause.<br>**中文：**可修饰动词、形容词、其他副词或整个分句。说明文常见方式 gently、程度 completely、时间 immediately、频率 regularly；老师示例中的 simply 也是副词。并非所有副词都以 -ly 结尾。 | **EN:** Can specify manner, degree, timing or frequency, or frame a statement.<br>**中文：**可补充怎样做、做到什么程度、何时做、多久一次等；作用取决于具体副词。 | **EN:** **Gently** turn the screw until it feels secure.<br>**中文：****轻轻地**转动螺丝，直到感觉已经固定好。 | **EN:** The manner adverb “gently” modifies “turn”, limiting the force users should apply. Alongside “until it feels secure”, it gives beginners both a method and a stopping cue, reducing the likelihood of overtightening and damaging the screw.<br>**中文：**方式副词 gently 限制转动时使用的力度；配合 until 给出的停止依据，既告诉新手怎样做，也提示做到何时，减少拧得过紧而损坏螺丝的风险。 |
| **Second-person personal pronoun｜第二人称人称代词** | **EN:** A personal pronoun referring to the person or people being addressed: you.<br>**中文：**you 直接指向读者或听者，单复数形式相同。second-person pronoun 是词类细分；direct address 是相应的交际用法，不是词类。 | **EN:** Addresses readers directly and assigns them a role.<br>**中文：**直接面向读者，让读者成为操作的参与者或执行者。 | **EN:** **You** can check the battery level on the screen.<br>**中文：****你**可以在屏幕上查看电池电量。 | **EN:** The second-person pronoun “you” directly addresses the user and makes them the person able to perform the check. In a guide read without an instructor present, this creates a clear link between the written explanation and the reader’s own action.<br>**中文：**you 直接指向使用者，把读者设为能够完成检查的人。在没有指导者在场的阅读情境中，它把书面说明与读者自己的行动直接联系起来。 |
| **Possessive determiner｜物主限定词** | **EN:** A determiner placed before a noun to indicate possession or association, such as your or its.<br>**中文：**放在名词前表示所属或关联，如 your device、its cover。your 是限定词；可独立使用的 yours 才是物主代词。 | **EN:** Connects an object or action to its owner or associated participant.<br>**中文：**明确物件属于或关联谁，使操作对应到读者自己的物件。 | **EN:** Save **your** work before restarting the computer.<br>**中文：**重启电脑之前，先保存**你的**工作。 | **EN:** The possessive determiner “your” associates “work” with the reader’s own material. This makes the potential consequence of restarting personally relevant, supporting the precautionary instruction by reminding users that their own unsaved work may be lost.<br>**中文：**your 将 work 与读者自己的内容关联起来，使重启的后果更切身；读者由此意识到可能丢失的是自己的未保存内容，更能理解预先保存的理由。 |
| **Article: definite / indefinite｜冠词：定冠词／不定冠词** | **EN:** A type of determiner: the usually marks an identifiable referent; a/an usually introduces a non-specific singular countable referent.<br>**中文：**the 通常表示在语境中能够辨认的对象；a／an 通常引入非特指的单数可数对象。冠词属于限定词，不是代词。 | **EN:** Distinguishes identifiable objects from non-specific items.<br>**中文：**区分读者应能识别的对象与不限定具体身份的物件。 | **EN:** Use **a** cloth to clean **the** screen.<br>**中文：**用**一块**布清洁**该设备的**屏幕。 | **EN:** The indefinite article “a” introduces a non-specific cloth, while the definite article “the” treats the device’s screen as identifiable. This contrast distinguishes a material users need to supply from the particular component they are instructed to clean.<br>**中文：**a 引入一块非特指的布；the 把设备屏幕作为语境中可识别的对象。两者帮助区分“需要准备的一件材料”和“要清洁的那个具体部件”。 |
| **Preposition｜介词** | **EN:** A word expressing a relationship, often of place, time or direction, and heading a prepositional phrase.<br>**中文：**常表达位置、时间或方向等关系，并构成介词短语的中心。如 under 是介词，under the cover 是介词短语；不要把单词和整个短语混为一谈。 | **EN:** Specifies a spatial, temporal or other relation.<br>**中文：**明确物件、动作或时间之间的关系。 | **EN:** Place the tray **beneath** the outlet.<br>**中文：**把托盘放在出口**正下方**。 | **EN:** The preposition “beneath” specifies the tray’s position relative to the outlet. It turns “place the tray” into a locatable instruction, helping readers position it where discharged liquid can be collected rather than merely somewhere near the device.<br>**中文：**beneath 明确托盘相对出口的位置，让“放置托盘”成为能够准确定位的指令，帮助读者把托盘放到能接住排出液体的位置。 |
| **Lexical / main verb｜实义动词／主要动词** | **EN:** A verb carrying the main lexical meaning of an action, event or state, rather than primarily supporting another verb.<br>**中文：**提供动作、事件或状态的核心意义。说明文常见 press、insert、remove 等动作动词；lexical verb 不只包括动作动词，也可表达状态。 | **EN:** Specifies the action or state central to the instruction.<br>**中文：**明确要执行什么动作或达到什么状态。 | **EN:** **Insert** the card into the slot with the label facing upwards.<br>**中文：**将标签面朝上的卡片**插入**卡槽。 | **EN:** The lexical verb “insert” names a precise physical action. Combined with the destination and orientation details, it gives inexperienced users an executable instruction, reducing uncertainty about both what to do with the card and how to position it.<br>**中文：**insert 指定明确的实际动作，再配合目标位置与朝向信息，把说明变成新手可执行的步骤，减少“不知道怎样处理卡片”的不确定。 |
| **Auxiliary verb｜助动词** | **EN:** A verb used with a main verb to help express tense, aspect, voice, negation or modality.<br>**中文：**配合主要动词表达时态、体、语态、否定或情态。基本助动词包括 be、have、do；情态动词也是助动词的一类。同一单词的作用要结合句子判断。 | **EN:** Helps construct the time, process, voice or force of a statement.<br>**中文：**帮助形成动作进行状态、语态、否定等意义，本身没有一套固定效果。 | **EN:** The device **is** charging; wait until the light turns green.<br>**中文：**设备**正在**充电；请等到指示灯变绿。 | **EN:** The auxiliary “is” combines with “charging” to form the present progressive, presenting charging as an ongoing process. The instruction to wait for a green light therefore has a clear basis: the device has not yet reached the indicated completion point.<br>**中文：**助动词 is 与 charging 构成现在进行体，表明充电仍在进行。这为等待绿灯的要求提供理由：设备还没有达到文本所规定的完成状态。 |
| **Descriptive adjective｜描述性形容词** | **EN:** An adjective describing an attribute, such as colour, size or shape, rather than primarily expressing an evaluation.<br>**中文：**主要描述颜色、大小、形状等属性，如 red、small、round；与老师清单中的 evaluative adjective 对照。某些形容词在不同语境中也可能兼有评价意味。 | **EN:** Provides attributes that help identify or specify an item.<br>**中文：**提供可用于辨认物件或判断要求的具体属性。 | **EN:** Connect the cable to the **blue** socket.<br>**中文：**把线缆接到**蓝色的**接口上。 | **EN:** The descriptive adjective “blue” gives a visible attribute for selecting the correct socket. For beginners who may not recognise technical port names, this colour cue makes the instruction more accessible and reduces the chance of connecting the cable incorrectly.<br>**中文：**blue 提供看得见的选择依据；对可能不认识专业接口名称的新手，颜色提示能降低辨认门槛，减少接错线缆的可能。 |
